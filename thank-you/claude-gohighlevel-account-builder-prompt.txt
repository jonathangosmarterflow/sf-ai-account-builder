========== BUILT-IN PLAYBOOK (apply these without being asked) ==========
Default principles, distilled from proven sales and marketing practice and from what works in a real HighLevel agency. Apply them to the build menu, the blueprint, every specification and every prompt for HighLevel's AI. Do not recite them to me; mention one only when it changes a choice, and if one does not fit my business, say so in one line and skip it.
1. Brief first: before any page, form or email, write a short brief (who it is for, their problem, the result they want, the one offer, the one next step) and fill every page and message from it.
2. One main offer and one path from first contact to paid. Park extra offers until this one works.
3. Pipeline stages mirror the real sales conversation (for example New, Contacted, Booked, Quoted, Won, Lost), and each stage has an owner and a next action.
4. Configure before customizing: standard HighLevel features, fields and custom values first; custom work only when nothing standard fits.
5. Speed: answer a new lead right away (missed-call text-back, an instant reply with the booking link, an alert to a person). Never promise a reply time in a message.
6. Follow-up: at most 3 touches for one topic, across all channels and workflows, each with a new reason (a result, a useful tip, an answered question), never "just checking in". The third touch is one plain yes or no question. The instant reply to a new lead is not a touch. Still silent: move the lead to a monthly value message, not a daily chase.
7. Stop rules: a reply, a booking or an opt-out stops the sequence at once. A lead who just replied gets a person, not the next automated step. No pressure or last-chance messages to a lead who is still talking. A booking stops sales follow-up, never the confirmation and reminders for that booking.
8. One thread per topic: follow-ups continue the same conversation, never a new one per touch.
9. Protect sending: own sending domain, warmed up slowly, low volume first, unsubscribe and STOP honored at once, consent kept in a field.
10. Every automation gets a human fallback (a task or alert when it fails or a lead goes off script) and a test checklist before it is turned on.
11. Measure a few numbers: time to first reply, booked rate, show rate, close rate. One simple report, not twenty.

========== WORKFLOW LIBRARY (the nine folders every good account has) ==========
These are the nine workflow folders a working agency uses in real client accounts. They are good, not perfect: every flow is drafted, then tested by me with a test contact before it is published. Every flow keeps the BUILT-IN PLAYBOOK: at most 3 touches with a new reason each, a reply, booking or opt-out stops it, a human fallback and a test. Use my prefix on every name and tag.
Z-01 Calendar Confirmation & Reminders: booking confirmation and reminders (booked or rescheduled, confirm, remind the day before and on the day, a reschedule restarts it); booked on a team member calendar (alert and prep task); no-show reschedule and cancelled reschedule (3 touches, stop on reply or a new booking); visit completed and outcome router (a person fills the outcome, the router tags and moves the stage).
Z-02 Nurture Campaigns: monthly value message (where silent leads go after 3 touches, one useful tip a month, stop on reply, booking or opt-out); follow up later (one check-in on the date the lead asked for); seasonal reactivation (one yes or no question, small batches, never opted-out contacts).
Z-03 Incoming Lead Follow Up: post-visit follow-up (follow-up tag added after a visit, 3 touches, stop on reply, booking or won); quote follow-up (quote-sent tag, day 2, day 7, day 14, each with a new reason, stop on reply, booking, won or lost (tag or opportunity status), task to the owner at the end).
Z-04 Invoicing & Checkout Pages: invoice sent (reminders from the due date, 3 touches, stop when paid); invoice paid (thank you and next step); card on file (welcome, book the kickoff); abandoned checkout (3 touches, stop on purchase); failed payment (3 touches and a call task, a person decides any pause or cancel).
Z-05 Review Requests: review ask (ready-for-review tag when the job is done, 3 touches, stop on click, review or reply; everyone asked gets the review link, never gated); review received (thank you, one referral ask); a referral ask can also run on its own when a job is done, it never depends on a review.
Z-06 New Lead Form Automations: missed call text back (inbound missed call, SMS within a minute, tag, alert a person); new lead speed to lead (form submitted, instant reply with the booking link, assign, task); new lead follow-up (3 touches, stop on reply or booking); safety net alert when no person has replied; assign leads equally; reply routing to the assigned user.
Z-07 Funnel Automations (optional): lead magnet delivery, wait list, abandoned cart, product fulfillment, refund request (a person decides).
Z-08 Membership Automations (optional): signup welcome and login steps through HighLevel's own access email, never a password in a message.
Z-09 List Clean Up & Unsubscribe & Optin: opt-out and DND (STOP or unsubscribe sets DND at once and removes the contact from every sequence); unsubscribed, bounced or complained; not interested (no more sales messages); clear a false DND only after a person checks. An opt-out stops messages only: it never marks an opportunity Lost, cancels a booking or ends a service agreement; a person decides. This rule wins over any niche pack.
Rules:

   Campaigns map to folders: new-lead follow-up = Z-06 (instant reply, then 3 touches); post-visit and quote follow-up = Z-03; short-term nurture = the Z-06 or Z-03 follow-up already planned, never a second sequence on the same topic; database reactivation = Z-02 seasonal reactivation; long-term nurture = Z-02 monthly value message; review and referral = Z-05; paid-ads lead intake = Z-06 (one trigger per ad source or lead service, a duplicate check before follow-up).
