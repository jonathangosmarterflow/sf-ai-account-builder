How to start

  1. Click Copy the Account Builder above.
  2. Open a new chat in Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini, paste it and press Enter.
  3. Answer a few questions, or paste or upload what you already have (your website text, an offer doc, notes). You get your plan. Never paste a password or key into the chat.

The three packs

Your plan keeps everything in HighLevel by default. If you choose to build pages outside HighLevel, or run a workflow in a cloud worker, copy that pack and paste it into the same chat when you reach that item. The third pack is optional: after the read-back, SHOW IT turns what was built into a one-page demo for your team, a client or a lead. New packs, not yet run end to end by us.

Later, when your plan is ready
