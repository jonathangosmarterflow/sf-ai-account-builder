Questions

Does it work with ChatGPT?

Yes, in Planning Mode. ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude produce the blueprint, the specs, the prompts for HighLevel's own AI and the human checklist. Planning Mode makes no changes in HighLevel. Automatic creation needs Claude.

Is this the official route?

Yes. Connected Build uses HighLevel's official connection, which you sign in to. No captured session tokens, no internal app endpoints, and you never paste a password or key into the chat.

Will it change my existing account?

Only what you approve. It is built to confirm the exact sub-account, preview every change and wait for your go before it writes anything. The prompt tells Claude not to delete or overwrite anything and to skip a name that already exists. You check the change list and the read-back.

What can it not build?

Supported workflows, funnels, pages and forms are built by HighLevel's own AI from the prompts it writes (anything it cannot build gets plain specs), and you review them before publishing. SMS templates, snapshots, phone numbers, messaging registration, domains and DNS, payment connections and connected Google or social accounts stay with a human. It does not run your marketing or promise results.

Do I need to know HighLevel?

It is beginner friendly, and you review everything before it goes live. Knowing your way around a sub-account helps.
